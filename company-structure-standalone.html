How we're built

Four rules that keep twenty people feeling like fifty

Lean by design

Twenty seats, each a multiplier. Every leader still does the work — the GM inspects homes, the CTO ships code, the CEO answers guest mail on Sundays.

Short lines to the guest

No one is more than two steps from a guest conversation. Leaders carry a pager, not just a plan — every review and every incident reaches the top within a day.

Owners, not approvers

Each team owns its numbers end-to-end — budget, vendor, outcome. Autonomy with audit: the monthly scorecard is open to everyone, no exceptions.

One table

Like jollof rice, everyone contributes to one pot. Monday all-hands, one shared roadmap, one standards book — hierarchy decides, never divides.
