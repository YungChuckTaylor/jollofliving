## 1. Purpose and how to read this pack

This pack translates the **live product economics already encoded in the Jollof Living platform** (12% host commission / 88% host payout, 8% guest service fee, 7.5% VAT, ₦15,000 cleaning fee, 20% security deposit held in escrow, weekly/monthly length discounts, experiences, gift cards, referrals, 8% affiliate commission, and corporate billing) into a professional three-horizon forecast.

Year 1 is treated as the first full operating year after go-live (calendar 2027), with 2026 as a partial launch / build year.

## 2. Business model (from the product)

### 2.1 What Jollof Living sells

A **two-sided marketplace** with escrow:

- Guests book verified luxury homes (penthouses, villas, suites) in Lagos & Abuja, with Instant Book or request-to-book.
- Hosts keep **88%** of the lodging amount; Jollof takes **12%** commission, weekly escrow payouts, WHT computed on host payouts (product: ~5% remitted to FIRS — **not company revenue**).
- Guests pay an **8% service fee** on (lodging after length discount + add-ons) plus **7.5% VAT** (collected and remitted — **not company revenue**) and a **₦15,000 cleaning fee** (pass-through to operations / host, modelled as 100% pass-through).
- **Security deposit 20%** is pre-authorised and held; not GMV, not revenue.
- **Add-ons** (transfers, chef, insurance, carbon) sit on the booking; insurance/carbon are %-of-subtotal; chefs/cruises are also sold as standalone **experiences**.
- **Length discounts:** −12% weekly (7+ nights), −25% monthly (30+) with 50/50 split pay — these reduce GMV and therefore fees.
- **Jollof Club** (Bronze→Platinum) is a loyalty engine (points at 5×–higher multipliers), not a paid membership in the current product.
- **Gift cards** and **₦10,000 / ₦10,000 referrals** are balance-sheet / marketing, not core margin.
- **Affiliate programme:** 8% of referred booking GMV — a **cost of sale**.
- **Corporate:** centralised invoicing, PO numbers, policy caps — higher ADR, longer stays, slower sales cycle.
- **Host tools:** photography, AI pricing, channel sync (Airbnb / Booking.com / VRBO) — near-term these are **acquisition costs**; from Year 4 a thin **SaaS / co-host fee** is introduced in the model.

Payments (Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe, cards, transfer, USSD, mobile money) are architected but currently **record-only / escrow-state**. Forecasts assume a live gateway from Year 1 with **1.5% blended PSP cost** on captured guest payments.

### 2.2 Unit economics (illustrative stay)

Typical seed ADR is **₦145,000–₦420,000**; host estimator defaults to **₦150,000/night at 65% occupancy**. Use a **base ADR of ₦185,000** (Onyx-class penthouse) and a **3-night Instant Book** with no add-ons, no promo:

| Line | Amount (₦) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Lodging (3 × 185,000) | 555,000 | |
| Cleaning (pass-through) | 15,000 | |
| Guest service fee 8% of lodging | 44,400 | Platform revenue |
| VAT 7.5% on lodging + cleaning + service | 46,080 | Remitted |
| **Guest total (ex-deposit)** | **660,480** | Checkout |
| Host commission 12% of lodging | 66,600 | Platform revenue |
| Host payout 88% of lodging | 488,400 | Before WHT |
| **Platform revenue (commission + service)** | **111,000** | **20.0% of lodging** |
| PSP 1.5% of guest total | (9,907) | |
| **Contribution before CAC / opex** | **~101,000** | **~18.2% of lodging / ~15.3% of guest total** |

**Rule of thumb used below:**  
**Net take (after PSP, before VAT/WHT) ≈ 17.5% of lodging GMV** in Year 1–3, rising to **18.5%** as add-on/experience mix grows, then **19.5%** by Year 10 with SaaS.

Experiences (cruise ₦85k, chef from ₦55k) are modelled at **25% platform take** after operator payout.

## 3. Market frame (planning, not a census)

- Nigeria’s short-let / serviced-apartment demand is concentrated in **Lagos Island / Lekki / VI / Ikoyi** and **Abuja (Maitama, Wuse, Jabi)** — exactly the neighbourhoods in the product.
- Luxury nightly rates on the catalogue (**₦90k–₦500k+**) sit above mass Airbnb and below five-star hotels on a per-key, full-home basis.
- Detty December, Easter and Eid are already in the pricing engine (+18% to +25%); occupancy is **seasonally peaked**, not linear.
- Competitive set: Airbnb, Booking.com, local operators, hotel apartments. Jollof’s stated edge is **escrow, KYC, in-person verification, local concierge, NGN rails, long-stay lease**.
- Expansion logic in the 10-year case: **Port Harcourt → Accra → Abidjan → Nairobi** (English/French, same luxury African positioning).

### 6.4 Cohort economics by Y5 (base)

- **Guest LTV:** 3.1 stays × ₦980k average basket × 17.5% net take ≈ **₦535k LTV** vs **₦12k CAC** → LTV/CAC **~45×** on loyal luxury travellers (small absolute numbers; do not over-scale paid social).
- **Host LTV:** ₦26.8bn lodging / 500 homes ≈ ₦54m GMV/home/year × 12% × 4.5 year remaining life ≈ **₦29m** commission per home vs **₦0.3m** onboarding → the constraint is **trust and supply quality**, not CAC.
- **Payback on a new listing:** < 2 months of occupancy at 55%.

### 6.5 Five-year risks that move the number

1. **Naira path** — ADR is Naira-quoted; USD GMV can stall even if Naira GMV grows.
2. **Airbnb price war** in Lekki — defend with escrow + concierge, not discounts.
3. **Payments / CBN** — escrow licensing; model assumes partnership with a licensed PSP, not a full bank licence.
4. **Detty December concentration** — >30% of annual GMV in 6 weeks without corporate/long-stay mix.
5. **Trust event** — one high-profile safety failure can freeze supply growth for a year (conservative case).

## 11. Interpretation for founders and investors

1. **This is a high-gross-margin marketplace** (~80% on net revenue) with **thin absolute GMV** until listing count breaks ~150. Do not hire like a consumer super-app in Year 1.
2. **The 12% / 8% fee stack is the whole company** until SaaS. Protect it; discounting to win hosts is how the conservative case happens.
3. **Luxury is a feature, not a TAM slide.** 2,200 homes at ₦310k ADR is ambitious but still a **niche** versus mass short-let. That is the point: trust, escrow, concierge.
4. **Naira vs dollar.** Raise and report in **both**. A beautiful Naira P&L can be a flat USD story.
5. **Demo GMV (₦412m/month) is not the plan.** That would imply ~₦5bn/year from day one — roughly **Year 3 base** in this pack. Using demo admin numbers in a pitch would be misleading.
6. **Payments go-live is the real Year 0 milestone.** Until then, forecasts are product theatre.

## 12. Method notes

- Catalogue ADR band and 12% / 8% / 7.5% / ₦15k cleaning / 20% deposit / 88% host payout / 8% affiliate / ₦10k referral / experience prices / occupancy estimator (65% host-side) are taken from the Jollof Living application (`Pricing`, host dashboard, membership, payments, seed listings).
